About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is designed to help you capture your thoughts, tasks, and ideas instantly without any distractions. It provides a clean, minimalist interface that lets you focus on what matters most: your notes.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Click the "New Note" button on the dashboard.
  2. Type your note or paste your text into the editor.
  3. Click "Save" to automatically store your note in the cloud.

Features

  • Lightning-fast search functionality.
  • Cloud synchronization across all your devices.
  • Markdown support for rich text formatting.
  • Dark mode for late-night brainstorming.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N Create a new note
Ctrl + S Save the current note
Ctrl + F Search through notes
